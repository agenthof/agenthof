# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Agenthof is an open project that welcomes contributors and users regardless of
background or identity. We are committed to a harassment-free, respectful, and
collaborative community.

## The standard

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its code of
conduct. The full text is canonical and available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: assume good faith, be respectful and constructive, welcome newcomers,
and focus on what is best for the community and the project. Harassment,
discrimination, personal attacks, and other unacceptable behavior described in
the Covenant are not tolerated in project spaces (the repository, issues, pull
requests, discussions, and any other official channel).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to **security@agenthof.dev**. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer will review and respond, and may take any action
appropriate to the circumstances, up to and including a ban from project spaces.

Because Agenthof is maintained under a pseudonym, enforcement is carried out by
the maintainer through this contact; it does not depend on, and will not
require, disclosing any reporter's or maintainer's real identity.
